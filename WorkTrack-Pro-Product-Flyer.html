Getting Started

Up and running
in 30 minutes.

No servers to configure. No IT department needed. One person sets it up, everyone benefits.

1

Build & Distribute

Run one command to build a .exe installer. Share it via WhatsApp, email, or Google Drive. Employees install it in under 30 seconds — no admin rights required.

2

Admin Configures Once

Set your office location, WiFi network name, working hours, and grace period. Saved to the cloud instantly — all employees get the settings automatically. Takes 5 minutes.

3

Team Checks In Daily

App launches with Windows. Employee opens it, clicks Check-In. Location is detected automatically. Admin sees it on the Overview in real-time. That's the entire daily workflow.

Attendance Intelligence

The Score that actually
tells the full story.

Not just "present or absent." A 100-point score that weighs three dimensions of attendance — because showing up is only part of the picture.

40 pts
Consistency
Days present out of total working days. Showing up regularly is the foundation of everything.
35 pts
Punctuality
On-time arrivals out of days present. Configurable grace period so one slow morning doesn't wreck the score.
25 pts
Office Presence
In-office days vs WFH days. Weighted to reflect your company's emphasis on in-person collaboration.
90–100 · Excellent
75–89 · Good
60–74 · Fair
0–59 · At Risk

Every technology choice was made for reliability, zero maintenance, and no licensing fees.

Electron 32

Native Windows desktop app. Runs offline-capable, boots with Windows, lives in the system tray.

React 18

The same UI framework powering Facebook and Notion. Fast, reliable, proven at scale.

Supabase

Cloud PostgreSQL database hosted in Mumbai. Free tier handles thousands of employees. Auto-backups included.

Pixel-perfect UI styling. Light mode, dark mode, and smooth transitions all built in.

GSAP Animations

ExcelJS

Beautiful, multi-sheet Excel reports generated locally in the app. No cloud export service needed.
